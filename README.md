# Real Estate Market Trends

## 📌 Project Overview

**Real Estate Market Trends** is a data analytics and visualization project developed as part of my internship at **SAM AI Technologies**.

The project focuses on analyzing real estate market data to identify important patterns related to property prices, rental yields, demand and supply, and geographical variations. Interactive visualizations are used to transform raw data into meaningful and actionable insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze property price variations across different locations.
* Compare rental yields to understand investment potential.
* Examine demand and supply trends in the real estate market.
* Identify geographical patterns using maps and heatmaps.
* Present complex real estate data through clear and interactive visualizations.
* Generate insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## 📊 Key Analysis

### 1. Property Price Analysis

Analyzes property prices across different locations and categories to identify pricing patterns, variations, and market trends.

### 2. Rental Yield Comparison

Compares rental yields across different properties and locations to understand potential rental returns.

### 3. Demand vs. Supply Trends

Examines the relationship between property demand and available supply to identify areas with higher or lower market activity.

### 4. Geographic Analysis

Uses geographical maps and heatmaps to visualize property prices, demand, supply, and other market indicators across different regions.
